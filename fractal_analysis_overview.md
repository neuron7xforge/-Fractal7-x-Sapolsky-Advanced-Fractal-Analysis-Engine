# 📌 TARGET, PURPOSE, BENEFITS, AND SOURCES

## TARGET USERS
- Quantitative researchers in finance and trading
- Neuroscientists analyzing EEG/fMRI/BCI signals
- Cognitive AI developers (e.g., Neuron7X)
- Complex systems analysts (climate, chaos, behavior)
- Algorithmic traders and hedge fund engineers
- Academics working on fractals, scaling laws, criticality

## PURPOSE
- Detect deep structure, scale invariance, and complexity in time series and signals
- Reveal hidden memory, multifractality, and critical transitions
- Enable real-time analysis and decision support in dynamic systems
- Provide a validated, interpretable diagnostic framework
- Serve as an autonomous module in cognitive-computational agents

## BENEFITS
- Quantifies complexity: beyond average or linear metrics
- Detects phase transitions, critical points, and regime shifts
- Provides indicators of market stability or cognitive overload
- Fully modular and deployable in production systems (BCI, HFT, forecasting)
- Scientifically valid, reproducible, and cross-domain
- Supports real-time or offline batch processing
- Compatible with GPU acceleration and large datasets
