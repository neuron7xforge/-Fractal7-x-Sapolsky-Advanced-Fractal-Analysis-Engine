## 👤 About the Author

I am an **independent researcher** working autonomously, outside of academic or corporate institutions. My journey began with a strong fascination for **neuroscience and cognitive dynamics** — how the brain interprets time, patterns, and chaos. Over time, I realized that many of these abstract processes could be **simulated and algorithmically expressed through Python**.

What started as a personal curiosity evolved into a **self-directed research initiative**, focused on transforming theoretical models — fractals, wavelets, entropy, memory — into interpretable and functional systems.

I work alone by choice, guided not by credentials but by exploration. This framework reflects my belief that **cognitive-level complexity can emerge from simple tools**, when understood with precision.

> I don’t follow a curriculum — I follow complexity, structure, and mathematical elegance.
